• Senior Citizen Rate Advantage: Senior citizen depositors aged 60 years and above are entitled to an additional interest rate of **0.50% p.a.** across all deposit tenures.
Section 2: Premature Liquidation and Penalties
• Premature Withdrawal Permitted: Customers may break or liquidate their Fixed Deposit at any time through the FinBase App.
• Premature Penalty: A penalty of **1.00%** is deducted from the contracted interest rate applicable for the period the FD actually remained open.
• 7-Day Minimum Holding Rule: If a Fixed Deposit is closed prematurely within 7 days from the booking date, **zero interest** is payable under RBI regulations.
Section 3: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) & Form 15G / 15H
• TDS Threshold: TDS is deducted at 10% if the total annual interest income across all deposits exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
• Non-PAN Penalty: If a valid PAN is not linked to the customer account, TDS is statutorily deducted at 20%.
• Zero TDS Exemption (Form 15G/15H):
• Depositors whose estimated total annual income is below the taxable threshold may submit Form 15G (for individuals below 60 years) or Form 15H (for senior citizens aged 60+) digitally via the FinBase App to avoid TDS deduction.
Section 4: FinBase Wealth — Mutual Funds & SIP
• Direct Mutual Funds: FinBase Wealth provides access to over 1,500+ Direct Mutual Fund schemes with **0% distributor commission**.
• Minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP): Start investing with as low as **₹100 per month**.
• Redemption Turnaround: Redemption proceeds are credited directly to your FinBase Savings Account within T+1 to T+3 business days depending on scheme category.
Section 5: FinBase Wealth — 24K Digital Gold and Silver
• Purity & Storage: 24 Karat 99.9% pure digital gold backed by physical bullion stored in secure, insured vaults managed by MMTC-PAMP.
• Minimum Purchase: Customers can buy digital gold starting from as low as **₹10**.
• Physical Delivery: Digital gold balances can be redeemed into physical minted gold coins/bars starting at 1 gram, delivered securely to the customer's doorstep.

Section 22: Explicit Product Exclusions & Unsupported Services Policy
To prevent customer confusion and combat financial scams, FinBase explicitly defines services that are strictly NOT offered or supported on
its platform:
• Cryptocurrency & Virtual Digital Assets: FinBase does **NOT** provide cryptocurrency trading, Bitcoin/Ethereum wallets, or virtual digital asset exchange facilities.
• Agricultural Property Loans: FinBase does **NOT** offer loans against agricultural farmland or rural mortgage loans.
• Speculative Intraday Tips: FinBase does **NOT** provide daily stock market intraday calls, F&O options tips, or speculative trading advice.
• Unregulated Chit Funds / Ponzi Schemes: Strictly prohibited under compliance governance.
Customer support assistants must clearly state that these services are unsupported and unavailable in the FinBase knowledge base.
