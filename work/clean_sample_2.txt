Section 1: UPI Limits and 24-Hour Security Cooling Period
• Standard Daily UPI Limit: Up to ₹1,00,000 per calendar day across peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers.
• Transaction Frequency Limit: Maximum 20 outbound UPI payments per rolling 24-hour window.
• First 24-Hour Cooling Period Security Restriction:
• Whenever a customer links a new bank account or resets their UPI PIN, NPCI security protocols enforce a strict cooling period.
• Outward UPI payments are restricted to a maximum cumulative limit of ₹5,000 for the initial 24 hours following the PIN reset.
• Full ₹1,00,000 daily limit automatically unlocks exactly 24 hours after PIN registration.
Section 2: Failed Transactions and Auto-Reversal Turnaround Time (TAT)
When money is debited from a customer's account but the transaction fails or the beneficiary does not receive the credit:
• Mandatory Auto-Reversal TAT: Under RBI and NPCI Harmonization of TAT guidelines, failed transactions must be automatically reversed and credited back to the customer's account within **T+2 business days** (where T is the transaction date).
• Delayed Reversal Compensation:
• If the reversal takes longer than T+2 business days, FinBase compensates the customer ₹100 per calendar day of delay.
• Compensation is calculated from the expiry of the T+2 window until the exact credit date, deposited directly into the customer's account without requiring any manual claim submission.
Section 3: Merchant Disputes, Chargebacks, and Provisional Credit
• Customers can dispute unauthorized, double, or incorrect merchant debits through the FinBase App under 'Transaction Details -> Report Issue' within 3 calendar days of transaction occurrence.
• For eligible merchant disputes, provisional credit is credited to the customer's savings account within 7 working days while bank arbitration proceeds.

Q001: My UPI transaction failed but money was deducted. When will I receive a refund? (Payment incident case
1) Under Section 2, failed UPI debits are automatically credited back within T+2 business days under RBI and NPCI guidelines. [Reference
Payments SOP Section: Clause PAY-SOP-001].
• Switch Route: NPCI UPI 2.0 Central Clearing Switch node 2.
• Reconciliation code: Auto-reconciled under batch job PAY-REC-0001.
• Verification reference: NPCI Universal Transaction Reference (UTR) 12-digit key.
• Dispute turnaround time: 48 business hours.
