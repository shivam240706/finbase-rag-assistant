Section 1: Introduction and Card Tiers Overview
FinBase issues credit cards on the Visa and RuPay payment networks. This handbook outlines the contractual terms, charges, credit limits, grace periods, and reward structures governing all card variants.
1.1 FinBase Neo Credit Card (Lifetime Free)
• Joining & Renewal Fee: Lifetime Free (Zero joining fee, Zero annual fee forever).
• Rewards & Cashback: Flat 1.0% unlimited cashback on all eligible retail POS and online e-commerce transactions.
• Exclusions: Fuel purchases, wallet reload transactions, cash withdrawals, and government utility payments do not earn cashback.
• Airport Lounge Access: No complimentary airport lounge access is included on the Neo tier.
• Foreign Currency Markup: 3.50% on all cross-border and foreign currency transactions.
1.2 FinBase Luxe Credit Card (Lifestyle & Travel)
• Joining & Annual Fee: ₹999 + 18% GST.
• Annual Fee Waiver: The annual fee for subsequent years is 100% waived if the total annual card spend exceeds ₹1,20,000 in the preceding membership year.
• Rewards: 2.0% cashback on online e-commerce transactions, 1.0% cashback on in-store offline POS swipes.
• Airport Lounge Access: 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits per calendar year (strictly capped at 1 visit per calendar quarter).
• Foreign Currency Markup Fee: Reduced markup of 1.50% on all international and foreign currency payments (industry standard is 3.50%).
1.3 FinBase Metal Credit Card (Ultra-Premium)
• Joining & Annual Fee: ₹4,999 + 18% GST.
• Annual Fee Waiver: Waived upon crossing ₹5,00,000 in annual retail spending.
• Rewards: 3.5% reward points on flights, hotels, fine dining, and international transactions; 1.5% on other retail categories.
• Lounge Privileges: Unlimited complimentary domestic airport lounge access for primary cardholder, plus 4 complimentary international lounge visits per calendar year via Priority Pass.
• Foreign Currency Markup Fee: Industry-leading 1.00% markup on international transactions.
• Concierge: 24/7 global lifestyle and travel concierge desk.
Section 2: Credit Limit Assignment and Enhancement
• Initial credit limits are determined based on bureau score, verifiable income, and existing credit lines.
• Automated Limit Enhancement: Evaluated every 6 months for accounts demonstrating 100% on-time minimum payment track record and credit utilization maintained below 40%.
• Temporary Limit Enhancements: Available for festive or medical emergencies through in-app approval for up to 30 days.
Section 3: Billing Cycle, Grace Period, and Minimum Amount Due (MAD)
3.1 Interest-Free Grace Period Cardholders enjoy an interest-free credit grace period ranging between 45 to 50 days, depending on transaction date.
The grace period is applicable ONLY if the Total Amount Due from the previous billing cycle was settled in full by the due date.
3.2 Minimum Amount Due (MAD) Calculation
• MAD is calculated as 5% of the total outstanding balance, or ₹250 (whichever is higher), plus all applicable taxes, EMI installments, and overdue amounts from prior cycles.
• Paying only the Minimum Amount Due keeps the account in good standing with credit bureaus but attracts finance charges on the remaining unpaid balance.
3.3 Finance Charges and Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
• Unpaid statement balances attract interest at 3.49% per month (effective 41.88% per annum), calculated from transaction date until full realization.
• Cash advances from ATMs do not enjoy any grace period; interest accrues from the timestamp of cash withdrawal at 3.49% per month.
