Section 1: Account Features and Minimum Balance Terms
• Zero Minimum Balance: The FinBase Digital Savings Account operates on a 100% zero-balance structure. There is no Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement.
• Zero Penalty: Customers face zero penalty fees or account maintenance charges for maintaining a low or zero balance.
• Account Opening: Paperless digital onboarding via Aadhaar OTP and Video KYC in under 5 minutes.
• Virtual Debit Card: Instant free virtual RuPay/Visa Platinum debit card issued upon account creation.
Section 2: Tiered Savings Account Interest Rate Slabs
FinBase calculates interest on daily end-of-day balances, credited quarterly to the account on March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31:
Daily Balance Slab Annual Interest Rate (p.a.) Calculation Basis Up to ₹1,00,000 3.50% p.a.
Daily ledger closing balance Above ₹1,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000 6.00% p.a.
Incremental balance above ₹1L Above ₹10,00,000 7.00% p.a.
Incremental balance above ₹10L Interest is compounded on a quarterly basis and deposited directly into the savings account.
Section 3: Electronic Fund Transfer Limits & Charges
• UPI Transfers: 100% Free. Daily cumulative limit of ₹1,00,000 with a maximum of 20 outbound transactions per rolling 24-hour window.
• IMPS (Immediate Payment Service): Free for all active account holders, up to ₹5,00,000 per calendar day.
• NEFT & RTGS Transfers: Completely free with zero transaction fees and no upper limit for Full-KYC accounts.
• ATM Cash Withdrawals:
• FinBase Partner ATMs: 5 free cash withdrawals per calendar month.
• Non-Partner ATMs: 3 free withdrawals per month in metro cities (5 in non-metro locations).
• Subsequent ATM cash withdrawals beyond the free allowance incur a fee of ₹21 + 18% GST per transaction.

Q001: What is the minimum balance required for a FinBase Digital Savings Account? (Operational inquiry 1)
Under Section 1, the account is a zero-balance account with no Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement and zero penalty for maintaining a zero balance. [Reference Savings Account Manual Section: Clause SAV-RULE-001].
• Account Classification: Retail savings tier SAV-CAT-02.
• Verification engine: Real-time ledger audit module SAV-AUD-0001.
• Statement proof: Visible in quarterly account statements and downloadable TDS certificates.
• Support resolution SLA: Immediate resolution via automated banking chatbot.
