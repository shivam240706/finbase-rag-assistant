Section 1: Executive Summary & Objective
FinBase Technologies Private Limited ('FinBase') operates a regulated digital lending platform in partnership with licensed Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Scheduled Commercial Banks.
The objective of this Personal Loans Master Policy is to provide transparent, standardized, and legally binding guidelines regarding underwriting criteria, loan tenure, interest pricing mechanisms, loan servicing, default penalties, and loan closure workflows.
This document serves as the single source of truth for both customer-facing interactions and internal underwriting operations.
Section 2: Borrower Eligibility Criteria
2.1 Age and Nationality Applicants must be Indian citizens residing within Indian territorial boundaries at the time of application.
• Minimum Age: 21 years completed at the date of loan application submission.
• Maximum Age: 58 years at loan application (or 60 years at the scheduled loan maturity date, whichever is earlier).
2.2 Employment and Monthly Income Thresholds
• Salaried Individuals:
• Minimum net monthly in-hand salary of ₹25,000 credited directly via bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS). Cash or bearer cheque salary disbursements are strictly inadmissible.
• Minimum continuous employment of 6 months with the current employer, and minimum 12 months total verifiable work experience.
• Self-Employed Professionals & Sole Proprietors:
• Minimum net monthly business income of ₹35,000, verified via the last 2 consecutive financial years of Income Tax Returns (ITR) with Computation of Income and audited balance sheets.
• Business vintage of at least 24 continuous months in the same line of business at the registered operating address.
2.3 Bureau Credit Score and Repayment History
• Minimum Score Requirement: A bureau score (TransUnion CIBIL or Experian) of not less than 720 is mandatory.
• Delinquency Constraints:
• Zero Days Past Due (DPD) occurrences across all active credit facilities over the trailing 12 months.
• No write-offs, settlements, or restructured loan flags on record within the last 36 months.
• Total existing Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) must not exceed 55% including the proposed FinBase EMI.

Section 4: Interest Rate Mechanics & Processing Fees
4.1 Annual Percentage Rate (APR) Personal loan interest rates are fixed throughout the contracted tenure, calculated on a monthly reducing balance method.
• Base APR Tier 1 (CIBIL 780+): 10.49% to 11.99% per annum.
• Base APR Tier 2 (CIBIL 750 to 779): 12.00% to 13.99% per annum.
• Base APR Tier 3 (CIBIL 720 to 749): 14.00% to 16.99% per annum.
4.2 Upfront Processing Charges
• Standard processing fee is 1.5% of the gross sanctioned loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹1,000 and a maximum of ₹15,000.
• Goods and Services Tax (GST) at 18% is applicable over and above the processing fee and is deducted upfront from the disbursed proceeds.
• Disbursal stamp duty and e-mandate registration fees (₹150 to ₹400 depending on state of execution) are borne by the borrower.
Section 5: Repayment, EMI Mandate, and Default Penalties
5.1 Automated Clearing Mandate
• All repayments must be executed via automated electronic mandates: NACH, e-NACH, or UPI AutoPay.
• EMIs are debited on the 5th calendar day of each month. If the 5th is a bank holiday, debit is initiated on the preceding working day.
5.2 Bounce and Overdue Penalties
• Cheque/Mandate Bounce Charge: ₹500 + 18% GST per bounce occurrence.
• Penal Interest: Overdue EMIs attract penal interest at 2.0% per month (24% per annum) calculated on the overdue amount from due date to actual realization date.
Section 6: Prepayment, Part-Payment, and Foreclosure Guidelines
Section 6.1: Part-Prepayment Terms
• Part-prepayment is permitted only after completing a minimum of 6 consecutive monthly EMI cycles from the disbursal date.
• Borrowers may part-prepay up to 25% of the current outstanding principal balance once per financial year without attracting any penalty.
• If part-prepayment exceeds 25% of the outstanding principal in a single financial year, an administrative charge of 1.0% + GST is levied on the amount exceeding the 25% threshold.
Section 6.2: Foreclosure Charges & Rules
• The applicable foreclosure charge is 3% of the outstanding principal if the loan is closed prior to completing 24 months of the repayment tenure.
• If the loan is foreclosed after completing 24 months, the foreclosure charge is reduced to 1.5% of the outstanding principal.
• Full foreclosure is strictly not permitted within the initial 6 months from loan disbursal under any circumstances.
• Foreclosure requests can be initiated directly via the FinBase App under 'Loans -> Service Requests -> Foreclose Loan'.
Section 6.3: No-Objection Certificate (NOC) and Lien Discharge
• Upon settlement of all principal, accrued interest, and foreclosure fees, an authenticated digital No-Objection Certificate (NOC) is generated within 3 business days.
• FinBase updates credit reporting bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark) within 15 calendar days from the date of final loan closure.

Section 22: Dispute Arbitration and Customer Grievance Redressal
FinBase enforces a strict 3-tier customer grievance escalation mechanism:
• Level 1 (Support Executive): In-app chat ticket or email to `support@finbase.com`. Resolution TAT: 48 hours.
• Level 2 (Grievance Officer): If unsatisfied with Level 1, escalate to `grievance.loans@finbase.com`. Resolution TAT: 5 working days.
• Level 3 (Principal Nodal Officer & RBI Ombudsman): Unresolved disputes beyond 30 days can be appealed directly to the RBI CMS portal (`cms.rbi.org.in`).

Q070: What processing fee is charged on loan approval? (Operational case 70)

• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-03.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0070.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 2 Underwriting Manager.

Q071: What is the foreclosure charge if I close my personal loan in 18 months? (Operational case 71)
According to FinBase Personal Loan Policy Section 4.2, because the loan is closed prior to completing 24 months, the applicable foreclosure charge is 3% of the outstanding principal balance plus 18% GST. [Reference Policy Section: Clause PL-REG-071].
• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-04.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0071.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 3 Underwriting Manager.
• Escalation SLA: 24 business hours for credit review.
Q072: What is the foreclosure charge if I close my personal loan after 26 months? (Operational case 72)
As per Section 4.2, if the personal loan is foreclosed after completing 24 months, the applicable charge is reduced to 1.5% of the outstanding principal plus 18% GST. [Reference Policy Section: Clause PL-REG-072].
• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-01.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0072.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 1 Underwriting Manager.
• Escalation SLA: 24 business hours for credit review.
Q073: Can I close my personal loan within 3 months of getting it? (Operational case 73)
No. Under Section 4.2 of the FinBase Personal Loan Policy, full foreclosure is strictly not permitted within the initial 6 months from the date of loan disbursal. [Reference Policy Section: Clause PL-REG-073].
• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-02.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0073.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 2 Underwriting Manager.
• Escalation SLA: 24 business hours for credit review.
Q074: How much part-prepayment can I make without paying any fee? (Operational case 74)
Under Section 4.1, you can part-prepay up to 25% of the outstanding principal balance once per financial year with zero penalty, provided you have paid at least 6 consecutive EMIs. [Reference Policy Section: Clause PL-REG-074].
• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-03.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0074.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 3 Underwriting Manager.
• Escalation SLA: 24 business hours for credit review.
Q075: What happens if my monthly EMI bounces due to insufficient funds? (Operational case 75)

• Applicability: All retail borrowers under loan tier code PL-04.
• Verification note: Checked automatically via core lending system rule PL-VAL-0075.
• Exception criteria: Any deviation requires approval from Risk Level 1 Underwriting Manager.
