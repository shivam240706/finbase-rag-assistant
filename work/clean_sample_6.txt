Section 1: Customer Identification and KYC Tiers
• Minimum KYC (Digital Onboarding):
• Completed instantly via Aadhaar OTP validation.
• Monthly credit transaction limit: ₹10,000. Annual cumulative limit: ₹1,00,000.
• Validity: Strictly valid for 12 months, within which Full Video KYC must be completed to prevent account restriction.
• Full KYC (Unrestricted):
• Completed via Video KYC (V-KYC) or in-person verification.
• Removes all balance and transaction limits; unlocks personal loans, credit cards, and wealth management.
Section 2: Officially Valid Documents (OVD) Accepted
In accordance with RBI Master Directions on KYC, FinBase accepts the following 5 Officially Valid Documents for identity and address
verification:
1. Aadhaar Card (Via UIDAI DigiLocker OTP or masked paper e-Aadhaar).
2. PAN Card (Mandatory for credit cards, personal loans, and deposits exceeding ₹50,000).
3. Indian Passport (Must be valid and unexpired).
4. Driving License (Smart card or verifiable DigiLocker document).
5. Voter ID Card (Issued by the Election Commission of India).
Section 3: Video KYC Process, Timings, and Prerequisites
• Operating Hours: Video KYC agents are available **Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST** (closed on national holidays).
• Prerequisites for the Customer:
• Original physical PAN card in hand (laminated original card; screenshots or photocopies are strictly rejected).
• A blank white sheet of paper and a black or blue pen for live on-camera signature capture.
• High-speed internet connection and active smartphone/laptop camera and microphone.
• Device geolocation (GPS) must be enabled to verify physical presence within India during the video session.
Section 4: Account Inactivity, Dormancy, and Reactivation
• Inactive Account: An account with no customer-initiated financial transactions for **12 continuous months** is classified as Inactive.
• Dormant Account: If no customer transactions occur for **24 continuous months**, the account automatically transitions to Dormant status.
• Reactivation Procedure: Reactivation cannot be performed over phone support. The customer must complete a 1-minute digital re-KYC self-declaration and video verification via the FinBase App.
Section 5: Electronic Fraud & Zero Liability Policy
FinBase enforces RBI Customer Protection Guidelines regarding unauthorized electronic banking transactions:
• Zero Liability (100% Refund):
• Applies when unauthorized fraud occurs due to contributory negligence, fraud, or deficiency on the part of FinBase.
• Applies in third-party breaches where the customer notifies FinBase within 3 calendar days of receiving the transaction alert.
• Limited Liability:
• If reported between 4 to 7 calendar days, customer liability is capped at ₹5,000 (or transaction value, whichever is lower).
• Beyond 7 Days:
• Customer liability is determined based on bank board-approved dispute review and case investigation.
Section 6: Emergency Account & Card Blocking Protocols
• Instant In-App Freeze: Open FinBase App -> Security -> Toggle 'Freeze All Cards' to immediately disable POS, online, and ATM transactions.
• 24/7 Dedicated Fraud Helpline: Call toll-free **1800-FIN-BASE (1800-346-2273)** or email `fraud-alert@finbase.com`.
